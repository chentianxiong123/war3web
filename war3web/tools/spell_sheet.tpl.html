How to read this. The harness parses English tooltip text and watches one target dummy, so it nominates suspects rather than convicting them. A spell marked nothing observed may still be doing something this bench cannot see — a knockback, a vision grant, an effect that only lands on a hero. One marked does what it says is doing the right kind of thing; the numbers are only checked where the tooltip states one. Read it as a worklist, top-down.
